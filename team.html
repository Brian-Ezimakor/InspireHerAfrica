Our Inspiring Team

Connecting early-career African women with experienced mentors and fostering a community of growth and empowerment.




Chidinma Oli

Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Chidinma is a public health professional with over 3 years’ experience in population health studies. Her previous research focuses on resolving key gaps in epidemiology, maternal and child health and fertility care to provide insights that will boost population health outcomes.

She is also keen on evaluating health interventions, evidence synthesis, causes, & impacts of diseases in populations, and project disease trajectories.

She earned her Master’s in Public Health at the University of York, United Kingdom. In 2022, she was nominated for an honourable mention award for the 2022 MAXQDA Research for change grant. She is also a member of the Fertility Care for All Global Team, and works as a Research Associate in Helpman Development Institute where she leads research on emerging public health issues in Nigeria.

Ojonugwa Abubakar

Co-Founder & Programme Coordinator

Ojonugwa is a bioinformatician and a One Health scientist with a background in microbiology. She is an alumna of the prestigious commonwealth shared scholarship. She currently analyses genomic data from national and global pathogen surveillance systems to provide insights into the evolution and spread of WHO-priority pathogens. She also serves as a training officer on the national committee of the UK model WHO where she contributes to equipping young professions with the skills required to pursue a career in Global health.

She is passionate about youth empowerment, especially of females, through education. She is the grants manager at the Building Nations Initiative and has served as the Vice principal and facilitator to GRAMS and LifePAD. She is also a Community Leader at ONE UK where she advocates for social justice.

Omobolanle Onilogbo

Engagement Officer

Omobolanle is a development communications specialist. She has worked in the development sector for several years with projects in the areas of Gender empowerment, Public health, Migration, and Agriculture. She has experience in digital & traditional media strategies and has executed online & offline media campaigns.
She has worked as the Communications Officer for Danish Refugee Council in Nigeria. She has also worked with donors & international organizations such as Malala Fund, Osiwa, International Organization for Migration, Federal Ministry of Labor and Employment, Palladium, and Federal Ministry of Agriculture & Rural Development, among others.
She holds a BSc. Degree in International Law and Diplomacy from Babcock University, and is currently pursuing an MA in Global Media and Digital Cultures in the School of Oriental and African Studies, University of London. Holds several certificates in storytelling for change, communication in a virtual age, marketing communications, etc.

Oreoluwa Richard-Ogunyale

Administrative and Technical Officer

Oreoluwa Richard-Ogunyale holds a degree in Microbiology from the University of Ibadan. She has a dynamic blend of skills spanning both the administrative and IT realms.
She is a budding professional in the tech industry, having worked as a software engineer at Guaranty Trust Bank in Lagos, Nigeria and holds a diploma in Frontend Engineering from AltSchool Africa.
