About Us

Our Vision

Our vision is to equip African women with the tools to passionately pursue their dreams, cultivate their skills, and emerge as influential leaders in their respective fields, through mentorship. We aim to be the foremost mentorship program for African women, setting the standard for empowerment & success across the continent.

Our Mission

We are on a mission to empower African women through comprehensive mentorship and the cultivation of a supportive, thriving community. We aim to guide African women on their journey to success, providing the resources and connections they need to achieve their goals & lead with confidence.

Our story

Something about us

Inspire Her Afrika was founded with the goal of providing mentorship to young women across Africa. As the organization evolved, we have now seen the need to expand our services to include not just mentorship but holistic support for women’s personal and professional growth, hence the name change to Inspiring Women Support and Development Initiative allows us to reflect this broader mission while keeping Inspire Her Afrika as our “household name.”

The Inspire Her Afrika Mentorship Program is a transformative initiative designed to empower early-career women (0-3 years of work experience) with key skills and mentorship opportunities to excel in their careers. it comprises two phases: a Bootcamp phase and a Main Mentorship phase.
The Bootcamp phase runs for one month, after which successful participants transition to the Main Mentorship phase for 3 months. The detailed timeline for the mentorship phase will be communicated to participants.
The program aims to build the capacity of African women by providing practical skills, networking opportunities and guidance to thrive in chosen career paths.
No this program is exclusively designed for early-career African women to African women to address their challenges and opportunities.
